Our Institution

About ICCBI

Immaculate Conception College of Balayan, Inc. carries its patroness's name through four levels of Catholic formation — Elementary, Junior High, Senior High, and College — including the STEM, ABM, HUMSS, and Technical-Vocational strands, and bachelor's programs spanning education, business, technology, and hospitality.

The school was entrusted to the Augustinian Recollect Sisters in 1935, and that congregation continues to guide its community life today.
