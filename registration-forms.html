Enrollment & Registration

Begin your journey with the ICCnian community. Follow our streamlined online and on-campus steps to secure your slot for the upcoming school year.

Official Enrollment Portal

CK Link / Essentiel

All new and returning students must process their enrollment through our official digital portal. Secure, fast, and designed for your convenience.

  • Online pre-registration and account creation
  • Real-time status updates and SMS notifications
  • Secure document and payment tracking
Go to Enrollment Portal
Enrollment Pathways

Choose your registration track.

Whether you are joining us for the first time or continuing your ICCnian journey, follow the specific steps below to complete your enrollment.

For New Students

First-Time Enrollees

  1. 1

    Pre-registration

    Go to the CK Link portal to begin your pre-registration.

  2. 2

    Fill-out Information

    Click the "Pre-registration" button, fill out the pre-enrolment information, and register.

  3. 3

    Save Credentials

    Register and securely save your account credentials for future access.

  4. 4

    Submit Requirements

    Submit the necessary documentary requirements to the ICC Registrar’s Office.

  5. 5

    Payment

    Settle your assessment fees at the Treasurer’s Office.

  6. 6

    Confirmation

    Wait for an SMS confirmation that you have been officially enrolled.

For Old Students

Returning ICCnians

  1. 1

    Update Records

    Go to the ICC Registrar to update your grade or year level for the upcoming term.

  2. 2

    Payment

    Settle your assessment fees at the Treasurer’s Office.

  3. 3

    Access Portal

    Open the CK Link page.

  4. 4

    Get Credentials

    Click the "Get Username/Password" button on the portal.

  5. 5

    Fill Details

    Fill out the necessary verification details requested by the system.

  6. 6

    Confirmation

    Wait for an SMS containing your official login accounts.

Preparation

Standard Enrollment Requirements

Prepare these common documents ahead of time to ensure a smooth registration process at the Registrar's Office.

Academic Records

  • Report Card / Form 137 (for transferees)
  • Honorable Dismissal (if applicable)
  • Previous school ID (for verification)

Personal Documents

  • PSA Birth Certificate (Photocopy)
  • Good Moral Character Certificate
  • Recent 2x2 ID Pictures (white background)

Important Notes

  • Requirements may vary by grade level or program.
  • Bring original copies for verification purposes.
  • Contact the Registrar for specific program needs.
