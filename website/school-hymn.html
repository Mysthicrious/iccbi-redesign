The ICC Hymn.

Sung at every flag ceremony, assembly, and graduation — the hymn is the melody every ICCnian carries for life, from the first day of Elementary to the last walk across the stage.

Immaculate Conception College of Balayan, Inc.

The ICC Hymn

As sung by the ICCnian community
Verse I

Hail the ICCnians singing,
May our friendship never fail.
Guided with kind untiring teachers,
In our prayers, works, and lives.
Through the happy days of our childhood,
And the restlessness of our youth,
To us they taught the virtues of our lady —
Love of God and love of the good.

✦
Verse II

When we are faced with troubles and hardships,
To her we give honor and glory.
Nothing but these our loyalty cheer,
To our alma mater dear.
Give us the courage to face them all,
And fear neither sword nor blood.
The ICCnians will answer the bugle's call —
For dear ICC, our country, and our God.

♫  Text as printed in the ICCBI worship guide  ♫
