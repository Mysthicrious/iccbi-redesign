✦ PAASCU · PACUCOA Accredited Programs

Four levels, one formation.

From first letters to licensure, every ICCnian follows a continuous line of Catholic education — Elementary, Junior High, Senior High, and College, each with its own accredited programs and formation focus.

Grades 1–6Elementary
Grades 7–10Junior High
Grades 11–12Senior High
12 ProgramsCollege
Level 01

Elementary Department

Grades 1 to 6 — foundational literacy, numeracy, and faith formation under the care of the Augustinian Recollect Sisters. Core values, catechesis, and community life woven into every school day.

PAASCU Level II Accredited
Core Subjects

Foundational Literacy & Numeracy

English, Filipino, Mathematics, Science, and Makabansa — built on DepEd's K–12 curriculum with daily Catholic values formation.

Faith Formation

Values & Catechesis

Regular liturgy, catechism, and Marian devotion woven into the weekly rhythm of the classroom.

Enrichment

Arts & Physical Education

Music, arts, and PE rounding out a well-formed elementary learner, in and out of the classroom.

Community

Christian Life

Classroom communities that practice the ICCnian values daily — Integrity, Collaboration, Compassion, Benevolence, and Innovation.

Level 02

Junior High School

Grades 7 to 10 — the full DepEd Junior High curriculum, delivered inside ICCBI's Catholic formation program. Students grow into leaders through student government, organizations, and structured guidance counseling as they prepare to choose a Senior High track.

PAASCU Level II Accredited
Curriculum

General Academic Formation

Eight learning areas: English, Filipino, Math, Science, Araling Panlipunan, MAPEH, Edukasyon sa Pagpapakatao, and TLE.

Leadership

Student Government & Organizations

Organizations and student government that put the Vision's call to Christian leadership into practice early.

Guidance

Formation & Counseling

Structured guidance counseling as students prepare to choose a Senior High track — Academic or Technical-Vocational.

Faith

Sacramental Life

Regular Mass, retreats, and outreach programs forming students in Augustinian Recollect spirituality.

Level 03

Senior High School

Grades 11 to 12 — students choose their track and begin specializing for college or career. ICCBI offers the full Academic Track plus a Technical-Vocational track with TESDA-aligned qualifications embedded in the curriculum.

Academic Track

3 Strands
  • STEMScience, Technology, Engineering & Mathematics. Foundation for licensure and technical degrees.
  • ABMAccountancy, Business & Management. For learners headed toward commerce and enterprise.
  • HUMSSHumanities & Social Sciences. For future educators, communicators, and public servants.

Tech-Voc Track

4 Specializations
  • CookeryKitchen fundamentals and food preparation skills.
  • BartendingBeverage service and bar management.
  • Food & Beverage ServicesProfessional dining and hospitality service.
  • Computer Systems ServicingHands-on hardware and networking competencies.
Level 04

College Department

Bachelor's degrees and associate programs spanning education, business, technology, and hospitality. Programs are accredited by PACUCOA and embed TESDA/TVET qualifications where applicable — so graduates carry both a diploma and industry certifications.

PACUCOA Level II

Bachelor of Elementary Education

Forming the next generation of Catholic-school educators for the elementary classroom.

PACUCOA Level I Formal

Bachelor of Secondary Education

Preparing secondary teachers with specializations across four fields.

Majors: English · Mathematics · Filipino · Social Science
Bachelor's Degree

Embedded: Bookkeeping NC III · Computerized Accounting (SAP)
PACUCOA Level II

BS in Business Administration

For learners headed toward commerce, enterprise, and management careers.

Bachelor's Degree

TESDA: Food & Beverage NCII · Bartending NCII · Cookery NCII
Bachelor's Degree

BS in Computer Science

Software, systems, and information technology for a growing digital economy.

Bachelor's Degree
