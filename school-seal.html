✦ Identity & Symbolism

The School Seal of ICCBI.

Every element of the school seal carries meaning — faith, nation, congregation, and devotion woven into one emblem.

Symbolic Elements

Anatomy of the seal.

The seal sits at the center — each meaning placed exactly where its symbol lives on the emblem.

Center of the Shield

The Cross

Occupies the center of the shield, the most important position in the field. It symbolizes the missionary character of the Congregation and of the authorities of the school.

Dexter Side

AR Sisters Emblem

On the dexter side of the shield is the emblem of the Congregation of the Augustinian Recollect Sisters, who have managed the school since 1935.

Sinister Side

Rosary & Lily

On the sinister side are the attributes of the Blessed Virgin Mary: the Rosary which forms the letter "M" stands for Mary, and the flower lily which means purity.

The Field

The Shield

Bears the national colors — Red, White and Blue — an indication that the institution is genuinely Filipino and expresses "Patria," the field of the shield.

The outer rim contains the college name — Immaculate Conception College — with Balayan, Batangas in the lower portion, where the college is located. The motto "Patria · Scientia · Virtus" embraces the shield.

The Motto

"Patria · Scientia · Virtus"

Three words embrace the shield — each a pillar of ICCBI's mission.

Patria Fatherland

Love of country and commitment to the Filipino people. ICCBI forms citizens who serve their nation with integrity.

Scientia Knowledge

Pursuit of truth through disciplined study. ICCBI builds sharp minds grounded in wisdom and put to good use.

Virtus Virtue

Excellence of character and moral strength. ICCBI forms Christ-centered leaders whose word and deed align.

Carry the Seal Forward

Become an ICCnian.

Join a community that lives out Patria, Scientia, and Virtus every day — from Elementary through College.
